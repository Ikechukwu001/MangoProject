## Why use these records?

In a busy hospital store or pharmacy, the same item may be issued many times a day to different wards. Writing a **full voucher for every small issue** would take too long. A **daily issue book** lets the store record each issue in a line, and a **consolidated issue voucher** adds up the totals for the ledger.

## The daily issue book

A **daily issue book** is a bound book in which **every issue** of the day is written, **in order**.

| Date | Unit | Requisition no. | Item | Quantity | Received by (signature) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 5 Oct | Ward A | R 21 | Paracetamol 500 mg | 100 | A. Eze |
| 5 Oct | Ward B | R 22 | Paracetamol 500 mg | 50 | B. Musa |
| 6 Oct | Ward A | R 25 | Paracetamol 500 mg | 150 | A. Eze |
| 7 Oct | Ward B | R 27 | Paracetamol 500 mg | 100 | B. Musa |
| 8 Oct | Ward A | R 30 | Paracetamol 500 mg | 200 | A. Eze |

**Rules**

- Write each issue **when it happens**, in ink.
- Each entry is backed by an **approved requisition**.
- The **receiving person signs** each line.
- Do not tear out pages. Mark errors with a single line, and initial them.
- At the end of the day, **total** the issues for each item and have the entry checked.

## The consolidated issue voucher

A **consolidated issue voucher** is a **summary** of the issues of **each item** over a **period**, such as a week or a month. It may show all units together, or one unit at a time.

| Item | Ward A | Ward B | Total issued |
|---|---|---|---|
| Paracetamol 500 mg | 450 | 150 | 600 |

### Worked example: preparing it

From the daily book above:

1. **Ward A:** 100 + 150 + 200 = **450**
2. **Ward B:** 50 + 100 = **150**
3. **Total issued:** 450 + 150 = **600**

**Check.** Add the quantities in the daily book in a different order: 100 + 50 + 150 + 100 + 200 = **600**. The two totals agree.

## Posting to the ledger and bin card

Use the consolidated total to bring the ledger up to date.

```
new balance = previous balance − total issued
```

**Example.** *The ledger balance at the start of the week was 1 000.*

- New balance = 1 000 − 600 = **400**

The **bin card** should be updated as each issue is made, so at the end of the week the bin card, the ledger and the shelf should all show **400**.

## Checks

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| Daily book against requisitions | Every entry has an approved requisition |
| Daily totals against the consolidated voucher | The totals agree |
| Ledger against the bin card | The balances agree |
| Ledger against the shelf | A count gives the same figure |
| Signatures | Every entry has a signature |

Any difference must be traced and corrected.

## Common faults

| Fault | Result |
|---|---|
| Entries made later from memory | Errors and missing issues |
| Adding errors | Wrong ledger balance |
| Missing signature | No proof of receipt |
| A day left out | Totals too low and the ledger too high |
| Voucher not posted | The ledger is out of date, and ordering decisions are wrong |
