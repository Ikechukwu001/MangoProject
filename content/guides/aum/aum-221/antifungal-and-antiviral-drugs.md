## Fungal infections

Fungi cause infections of the **skin, hair, nails, mouth and vagina**. Deep, serious infections occur mainly in patients with weak immunity.

| Infection | Example |
|---|---|
| Skin | Athlete's foot, ringworm of the body or scalp, groin infection |
| Nails | Fungal nail infection (onychomycosis), slow to treat |
| Mouth | Oral thrush (candidiasis), common in babies and patients using inhaled steroids |
| Vagina | Vaginal candidiasis (thrush) |
| Deep or systemic | Rare, serious, treated in hospital |

## Antifungal drugs

| Drug | Form | Uses and notes |
|---|---|---|
| Clotrimazole | Cream, pessary | Skin and vaginal infections |
| Miconazole | Cream, oral gel | Skin infections and oral thrush. **Interacts with warfarin** even when used as gel |
| Nystatin | Oral drops or suspension, pessary | Oral and gut thrush. Not absorbed from the gut |
| Fluconazole | Capsule, injection | Vaginal and oral thrush, and deeper infections. **Interacts with many drugs** |
| Griseofulvin | Tablet | Ringworm of hair and nails, **long courses**, taken with a **fatty meal**. Not in pregnancy |
| Terbinafine | Cream, tablet | Skin and nail infections |
| Ketoconazole | Cream, shampoo, tablet | Oral tablets can **harm the liver**, and use is restricted |
| Amphotericin B | Injection | Serious deep infections, in hospital |

### Counselling

- **Complete the course**, even if the skin looks better. Nail infections can need months.
- Apply creams to **clean, dry skin**, and a little **beyond** the edge of the rash.
- **Wash hands** after touching the area. Do not share towels.
- Keep skin **dry**, wear **cotton** clothes, and dry between the toes.
- For oral thrush, **rinse the mouth** after steroid inhalers, and clean **dentures**.
- **Pessaries** are inserted at night, and may damage latex condoms and diaphragms.
- Report **yellowing of the eyes or skin**, dark urine or severe tiredness (liver problems) with oral antifungals.
- **Pregnancy:** ask the pharmacist before using any oral antifungal.

## Viral infections

Viruses live inside human cells, so it is hard to find a drug that kills the virus without harming the cell. This is why **there are far fewer antivirals than antibacterials**, and each acts against particular viruses.

**Antibiotics do not work on viruses.** Many viral illnesses, such as colds and flu, get better without specific drugs, and rest, fluids and symptom relief are used.

## Antiviral drugs

| Drug | Virus | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Aciclovir | Herpes simplex (cold sores, genital herpes), chickenpox, shingles | Cream, tablets, injection. Drink plenty of fluids. Best started early |
| Oseltamivir | Influenza | Used in some cases, started early |
| Antiretrovirals | HIV | A special group. They are covered in AUM 321 |
| Hepatitis B and C antivirals | Hepatitis viruses | Specialist treatment |

## Prevention: vaccines

**Vaccines** are the best protection against many viral diseases, including **measles, polio, yellow fever, hepatitis B and rabies**. Vaccines need the **cold chain** (2 to 8 °C for most). See the lesson on stability and the cold chain in BDT 352.

## Other advice

- **Handwashing** and good hygiene reduce spread.
- Patients with symptoms of **serious viral disease** (high fever, rash, breathing difficulty, bleeding) need a **doctor** quickly.
- Do not give **aspirin** to children with a viral illness, because of the risk of **Reye's syndrome**.
