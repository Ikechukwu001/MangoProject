## Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sedative | Calms and reduces activity or excitement |
| Hypnotic | Produces or helps **sleep** |
| Anxiolytic (tranquilliser) | Reduces **anxiety** |

The same drug can act as a sedative at a low dose, and as a hypnotic at a higher dose.

## Benzodiazepines

| Drug | Typical use |
|---|---|
| Diazepam | Anxiety, muscle spasm, alcohol withdrawal, fits (injection or rectal), before procedures |
| Lorazepam | Anxiety, status epilepticus, before procedures |
| Bromazepam | Anxiety |
| Nitrazepam, temazepam | Insomnia |
| Midazolam | Sedation before procedures, and urgent control of fits |

**How they work:** they **increase the effect of GABA**, the brain's main calming chemical.

### Uses

- **Short-term** treatment of severe anxiety and insomnia.
- **Epileptic fits** and fits caused by fever or poisoning (diazepam, lorazepam, midazolam).
- **Muscle spasm.**
- **Alcohol withdrawal.**
- **Before surgery or procedures.**

### Side effects and cautions

- **Drowsiness**, confusion, poor memory, and **unsteadiness**, with the risk of **falls**, especially in the **elderly**.
- **Impaired driving** and work with machines, including **next-day drowsiness**.
- **Dangerous with alcohol, opioids** and other sedatives, since breathing can slow.
- **Not suitable** in severe breathing problems, and cautious in liver disease and in pregnancy and breast-feeding.
- **Paradoxical** effects in some patients (agitation, aggression).

### Tolerance, dependence and withdrawal

- **Tolerance** and **dependence** can develop in **2 to 4 weeks**.
- **Withdrawal:** anxiety, shaking, sweating, sleeplessness, headache, and sometimes **fits**.
- Therefore:
  - use the **lowest dose for the shortest time**, generally **not more than 2 to 4 weeks**;
  - **reduce the dose slowly** to stop;
  - **never stop suddenly** after regular use.
- They are **controlled drugs**, and are **misused** by some people for effect.

### Overdose

Drowsiness, slurred speech, confusion, slow breathing, and coma, **especially with alcohol or opioids**. Treatment is **supportive**, in hospital. **Flumazenil** can reverse the effect in selected cases.

## Other drugs

| Drug | Notes |
|---|---|
| Z-drugs (zolpidem, zopiclone) | Hypnotics. Similar cautions to benzodiazepines, with dependence and next-day drowsiness |
| Barbiturates (phenobarbital) | Now used mainly for **epilepsy**. Dangerous in overdose, and many drug interactions |
| Sedating antihistamines (promethazine, chlorpheniramine) | Short-term sleep help, and sedation in children in some cases. Next-day drowsiness |
| Buspirone | An anxiolytic that does not cause dependence in the same way, but acts slowly |
| Propranolol | Reduces physical signs of anxiety such as palpitations and tremor |
| Antidepressants | Used for long-term anxiety disorders |
| Melatonin | Used for some sleep problems, under advice |

## Advice for patients

- Take **exactly as prescribed**, and **only for the planned time**.
- **No alcohol.**
- **Do not drive** or use machines until you know how it affects you.
- **Do not stop suddenly** after regular use.
- Never **share** tablets, and **keep them locked** and out of reach of children.
- **Older people** should be extra careful because of falls.
- Try **non-drug help** for sleep and anxiety: regular sleep times, avoiding caffeine late in the day, reducing screen time before bed, relaxation, exercise, and **counselling**.

## The technician's role

- Supply **only on a valid prescription**, and keep exact records.
- **Alert the pharmacist** to repeated requests, early refills, or several prescribers.
- **Store** securely in the controlled drug cupboard.
- **Be kind**: people with anxiety and sleep problems need support, not judgement.
