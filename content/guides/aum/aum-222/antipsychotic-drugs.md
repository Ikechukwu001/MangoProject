## Psychosis and schizophrenia

**Psychosis** is a condition in which the person **loses contact with reality**. Typical features:

- **Hallucinations**: hearing or seeing things that are not there
- **Delusions**: fixed false beliefs
- **Disorganised speech and behaviour**
- **Reduced emotion, motivation and speech** (in schizophrenia)

**Schizophrenia** is a long-term illness that often starts in young adults. Psychosis can also occur in **mania**, severe depression, and with **drug or alcohol misuse**. It is an **illness**, and patients deserve **treatment and respect**. Stigma and wrong beliefs about causes often delay care.

## Antipsychotic drugs

| Group | Examples | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Typical (first-generation) | Chlorpromazine, haloperidol, fluphenazine | Effective and cheap. More **movement side effects** |
| Atypical (second-generation) | Risperidone, olanzapine, quetiapine, aripiprazole, **clozapine** | Fewer movement problems. More **weight gain and metabolic** effects |

**How they work:** they mainly **block dopamine receptors** in the brain, which calms psychotic symptoms. They also have other actions that explain side effects.

### Other uses

- **Mania** and severe agitation
- **Nausea and vomiting** (chlorpromazine, haloperidol)
- **Hiccups** that do not stop (chlorpromazine)
- **Severe anxiety or behaviour disturbance** in some cases, under specialist care

## Side effects

| Effect | Notes |
|---|---|
| Extrapyramidal effects | **Parkinsonism** (stiffness, tremor, slow movement), **dystonia** (sudden muscle spasms of the neck, face or eyes), **akathisia** (restlessness), and **tardive dyskinesia** (late-onset abnormal movements, often of the face and tongue) |
| Sedation and drowsiness | Common, especially with chlorpromazine and quetiapine |
| Weight gain, raised blood sugar and cholesterol | Mostly olanzapine and clozapine. Monitor weight and blood tests |
| Hormone effects | **Raised prolactin**: breast enlargement or milk, missed periods, sexual problems (risperidone) |
| Low blood pressure on standing | Dizziness and falls |
| Anticholinergic effects | Dry mouth, constipation, blurred vision |
| Heart rhythm changes (QT prolongation) | Rare but serious |
| Neuroleptic malignant syndrome | Rare emergency: high fever, rigid muscles, confusion |
| Sun sensitivity | Chlorpromazine |

### Treatment of movement side effects

- **Anticholinergic drugs** such as **procyclidine** or **benzhexol (trihexyphenidyl)** treat parkinsonism and dystonia.
- The dose of the antipsychotic may be **reduced**, or the drug **changed**.
- **Tardive dyskinesia** is not treated by these drugs, and may need a change of drug.

### Clozapine

- Used when other antipsychotics have **failed**.
- Can cause **agranulocytosis** (a dangerous fall in white blood cells). **Regular blood tests** are required before supply.
- Other effects: drooling, drowsiness, constipation, fast pulse, seizures, weight gain.
- **Never restart** without medical guidance if stopped for more than a few days.

## Depot (long-acting) injections

Examples: **fluphenazine decanoate**, **haloperidol decanoate**, long-acting risperidone, aripiprazole.

- The drug is released **slowly** over **2 to 4 weeks or longer**.
- Helps patients who **forget or refuse** tablets.
- Given by **trained staff** into a muscle.

## Adherence

**Stopping treatment** is the **commonest cause of relapse**. Reasons patients stop: they feel well, side effects, lack of insight, cost, stigma.

**How to help**

- **Explain** that the medicine **prevents relapse**.
- **Discuss side effects** with the pharmacist and doctor, rather than stopping.
- Use **simple regimens**, a **pill box**, and **family support**.
- Make sure the patient can **afford and obtain** the medicine.
- **Never stop suddenly.**

## Counselling

- Take **regularly**, even when well.
- **Drowsiness:** avoid driving until used to the medicine, and avoid **alcohol**.
- **Stand up slowly.**
- Report **stiffness, tremor, restlessness, abnormal movements, fever, or sore throat** (with clozapine).
- Watch **weight**, and eat healthily and exercise.
- Use **sun protection**.
- **Do not take** other medicines without checking.

## The technician's role

- Treat patients and families **kindly and without stigma**, and keep information **private**.
- **Check** that regular patients are collecting their supply, and tell the pharmacist if they **stop** coming.
- Make sure **clozapine** is supplied only when the **blood test result** is in order.
- Store **depot injections** as the label says.
