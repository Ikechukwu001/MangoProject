## Protozoa

**Protozoa** are single-celled organisms. Some cause disease in humans. **Malaria** is the most important, and is covered in its own lesson. This lesson covers the others.

| Infection | Organism | Main signs | Spread |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amoebiasis (amoebic dysentery) | *Entamoeba histolytica* | Bloody or mucus diarrhoea, cramps. May spread to the liver (abscess) | Contaminated food and water |
| Giardiasis | *Giardia lamblia* | Watery, foul-smelling diarrhoea, wind, bloating, weight loss | Contaminated water and food |
| Trichomoniasis | *Trichomonas vaginalis* | Frothy, smelly vaginal discharge, itching, painful urination. Men may have no symptoms | Sexual contact |
| African trypanosomiasis (sleeping sickness) | *Trypanosoma* | Fever, swollen glands, sleep disturbance, confusion | Tsetse fly |
| Leishmaniasis | *Leishmania* | Skin sores or fever, enlarged spleen | Sandflies |
| Toxoplasmosis | *Toxoplasma* | Often mild. Dangerous in pregnancy and in weak immunity | Cat faeces, undercooked meat |

## Nitroimidazoles

| Drug | Uses |
|---|---|
| **Metronidazole** | Amoebiasis, giardiasis, trichomoniasis, bacterial vaginosis, and **anaerobic bacterial infections** such as some dental, abdominal and pelvic infections. Also used with other drugs for *H. pylori* |
| **Tinidazole** | Similar uses, with a longer action, often as a shorter course |
| **Secnidazole** | Similar uses, often as a single dose |

**How they work:** inside the organism, the drug is changed into a form that **damages its DNA**, and the organism dies.

### Side effects

- **Metallic taste**, nausea, vomiting, loss of appetite
- **Dark urine** (harmless)
- Headache, dizziness
- **Furry tongue**, mouth soreness
- Rarely, **nerve problems** (tingling, numbness) with long courses, and fits

### The alcohol warning

Alcohol with metronidazole or tinidazole can cause a **disulfiram-like reaction**: flushing, headache, nausea, vomiting and palpitations. The label should say **"Avoid alcohol"**. The usual advice is to avoid alcohol **during treatment and for at least 48 hours afterwards**, but follow the label or the pharmacist.

**Hidden alcohol** in some herbal mixtures and liquid medicines also counts.

### Cautions

- **Pregnancy**: ask the doctor or pharmacist, especially in the first trimester. Short courses may be used when needed.
- **Breast-feeding**: ask.
- **Liver disease**: dose may need reducing.
- **History of blood disorders or nerve disease**: refer.
- Interactions: **warfarin** (raises bleeding risk), **lithium**, and some antiepileptics.

## Treating each infection

### Amoebiasis

- **Metronidazole** or **tinidazole** treats the amoebae in the gut wall and tissues, including liver abscess.
- A **luminal amoebicide** such as **diloxanide furoate** is often given afterwards to clear amoebae left in the bowel and **prevent relapse**.
- **Fluids and oral rehydration salts** are given for diarrhoea.

### Giardiasis

- **Metronidazole**, tinidazole or secnidazole, often as a short course. Another course may be needed.
- Wash hands, and boil or filter drinking water.

### Trichomoniasis

- **Metronidazole** or **tinidazole**.
- **Treat sexual partners at the same time**, even without symptoms, and **avoid sex** until both finish treatment and symptoms clear.
- Test for other sexually transmitted infections.

### Others

- **Sleeping sickness**, **leishmaniasis** and **toxoplasmosis** are treated by **specialists** with specialised drugs. Refer such patients promptly.

## Prevention

- **Wash hands** with soap after the toilet and before handling food.
- **Boil or filter** drinking water where the supply is doubtful.
- **Wash raw fruit and vegetables**, and cook food thoroughly.
- Use **latrines**, and keep **flies** off food.
- Use **condoms** and limit partners.
- Protect against **tsetse flies** and **sandflies** in affected areas.

## Counselling

- **Finish the course**, even if you feel better.
- **No alcohol** during treatment and after it, for the time advised.
- Take with or **after food** to reduce stomach upset.
- **Do not share** tablets with others.
- Persistent diarrhoea, **blood in stool**, fever, or **dehydration** need a doctor, especially in children.
- Do not use a **nitroimidazole in pregnancy** without advice.
